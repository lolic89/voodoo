Biografija

Vudu Popaj (Voodoo Popeye) je reper, pisac i autor iz Zemuna. Od prvog demo snimka 1992. snimio je šest albuma i preko 25 spotova, objavio šest knjiga i strip, i korežirao film u kom igra glavnu ulogu.

Nastupao je širom Srbije i bivše Jugoslavije — u Sloveniji 1999, Bosni 2000, Makedoniji 2001, Hrvatskoj 2005, i više puta u Crnoj Gori. Organizovao je brojne humanitarne koncerte i podržao proteste grupa i pojedinaca čija su prava bila ugrožena.

Since his first album he has shot more than 25 music videos, collaborated with many well-known musicians and performed throughout Serbia and the former Yugoslav republics: Slovenia in 1999, Bosnia in 2000, Macedonia in 2001, Croatia in 2005, and Montenegro more than once. He has organized many charity concerts and supported a number of protests by groups and individuals whose rights were violated.
